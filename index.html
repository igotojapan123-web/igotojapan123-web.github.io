How it goes

Five steps, and you always know which one you’re in.

The five working days start the day I have your text and photos — not the day you pay. That one detail removes most of what goes wrong in these projects.

  1. You write to me. Within 24 hours you get a plan: the sections I would build, in order, and — more usefully — what I would leave out and why.
  2. We agree it in writing. A one-page scope sheet, including a section titled “not included this time”. Nothing is designed before you confirm it.
  3. Day 3: a live preview URL. The real page on your phone, not a picture of a design.
  4. Day 4: one round of changes. Send everything in a single message and I do it all at once.
  5. Day 5: live on your domain. Plus the source code and a short note on editing your own text.
If anything is going to slip, you hear it from me the day it happens — not on day five.
