2. What is included

For Fix Three ($290): on your current site — WordPress, static HTML, or most builders — within 48 hours of your hosting/CMS login working for me: (1) https working and enforced: certificate set up through your host, http redirected to https, and every stylesheet, script and image loading securely so the padlock shows; (2) dead third-party widgets and blocked resources removed or replaced — Google+ buttons, AddThis, retired font services and CDNs, broken analytics tags; (3) a quote or contact form that accepts file attachments and delivers to your inbox, replacing mailto links, with a confirmation to the sender; plus the viewport line added if it is missing so text is readable on a phone. The 48 hours start when I can log in, not when you pay. If your platform makes any of the three impossible, I tell you before touching anything and refund in full. Not included: redesign, new copy, moving hosts, a responsive rebuild — those are the landing-page tier.

For a landing page ($1,290):

  • A page designed for your business — not a theme with your logo dropped in
  • Built with Next.js, fast on a phone, tested on real devices
  • An inquiry or quote form that delivers to your inbox, with file upload if you need drawings or photos
  • SEO basics, a shareable link preview card, and https set up properly on your own domain
  • One full round of revisions
  • The source code, and a short written note on how to edit your own text
  • 14 days of fixes after launch for anything that does not work as agreed

Not included unless it is written in the scope sheet: copywriting from scratch, logo design, stock-photo licences, paid fonts or plugins, domain registration fees, ongoing hosting or maintenance, and anything in the “not included this time” section of your scope sheet.

3. Payment

  • Landing page: paid in full up front by card through Gumroad. Delivery is inside five working days, so you see the result well within Gumroad’s own refund window.
  • Multi-page and member systems: not sold through Gumroad. You get a written scope sheet, you agree it by email, and I invoice the deposit. Work starts when the deposit clears. Source code and domain hand-over happen after the final payment.
  • Korean clients may pay by bank transfer in KRW at the exchange rate on the day of transfer. Bank details are given privately, never on a public page.
  • I do not accept cryptocurrency, payment after delivery, or payment split into more than three instalments.

4. Timeline

The five working days start on the day I have your text and photos — not the day you pay. If your materials arrive later, I tell you the new delivery date in writing the same day. If I do not, the delay is mine and section 7 applies.

  1. You write to me. Within 24 hours you get a plan: the sections I would build, in order, and what I would leave out.
  2. We agree it in writing — a one-page scope sheet with a section titled “not included this time”. Nothing is designed before you confirm it.
  3. Day 3: a live preview URL. The real page on your phone, not a picture.
  4. Day 4: one round of changes, sent in a single message.
  5. Day 5: live on your domain, source code delivered.

5. Revisions

One full round is included. “A round” means you send everything you want changed in one message and I do all of it at once. Changes that move outside the agreed scope sheet — a new section, a different structure, a second language — are quoted separately before I do them, and you can say no.

6. Cancellation and refunds

If you cancel, the refund depends on how far the work has gone. This is the standard freelance structure — a deposit reserves the time, and the rest pays for work already done.

Costs already paid to third parties on your behalf — domain registration, paid fonts, images, plugins, hosting — are not refundable at any stage. They are listed in your quote before you pay so there are no surprises.

For landing pages bought through Gumroad, Gumroad’s own policies also apply. If a refund is approved it goes back to the original payment method within five working days.

7. When it is my fault

Full refund, no ladder, no argument if any of these happen:

  • The first preview clearly does not match the scope sheet we agreed.
  • I miss the delivery date by six working days or more and you would rather not wait. You keep whatever has been built.
  • I take the job and then tell you I cannot do it.

Smaller delays that are mine: one or two working days late, you hear from me the day it happens and I apologise. Three to five days late, you choose either 10% off or one extra round of changes free.

A delay caused by waiting on your materials or your reply is not a delay on my side — but I will always have told you the new date in writing, and if I have not, it counts as mine.

8. Ownership

  • The source code and design become yours when the final payment clears. Until then the preview is for review only.
  • If a refund is issued, the transfer is cancelled and the work cannot be used.
  • I may show the finished site in my portfolio. If you would rather I did not, say so in the scope sheet and I will not.
  • Anything you give me — text, photos, logins — stays yours and is used only for your project. I do not publish screenshots from inside any member system, ever.

9. How to request a refund or raise a problem

Email flyready.team@gmail.com with the project name. You get a first reply within 24 hours saying it is being looked at — not a decision on the spot. A decision, with the reasons and the dates it is based on, follows within five working days. Everything is judged on what was written down: the scope sheet, the preview dates, the change requests. That protects you as much as me.

The short version. Fixed price. Written scope before any design. Five working days from your materials. One round of changes. Cancel before you send materials and get everything back; after the preview you have the work, so there is no refund — unless the problem is mine, in which case you get all of it back.
